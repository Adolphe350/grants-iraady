Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: Fund Nest is free. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It collects no personal data. Everything you save stays on your own device.

Who we are

Fund Nest is published by Iraady Ltd, a UK company (UK Company No. 16951380). Contact: info@iraady.com.

What is stored on your device

The grants you save, your notes, owner names, amounts requested, pipeline stages, checklists and reminder settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. We never receive them and we cannot see them.

What the app downloads

The app downloads public grant listings (titles, funders, deadlines and similar facts) from grants.iraady.com so you can browse and search them. Your saved grants and notes are not sent in these requests.

Like any website, our server may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address and time of request) to keep the service secure and running. We do not use them to identify you, and we do not link them to anything you save in the app.

Reminders

Deadline reminders are local notifications created on your device. The app does not use a push notification service and does not send us a notification token. The app asks for notification permission only when you first turn a reminder on.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we do not hold any.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Links, sharing and export

"Open funder's page" opens the funder's own website in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy. When you use Share or export your grants as a CSV file, the data goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Reset app data" in Settings, deletes everything the app stored on your device, including saved grants, notes, checklists and scheduled reminders. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Fund Nest is a tool for people and organisations who apply for grants. It is not aimed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
