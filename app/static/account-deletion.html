Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to request permanent deletion of your access credentials and any personal data associated with the Fund Nest app (io.kodular.irankundaadolphe.FundNest) and the Grants Hub service at grants.iraady.com.

Deletion is permanent

Once your access and data are deleted they cannot be recovered. If you only want to stop using Fund Nest, you can simply sign out or uninstall the app instead.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@iraady.com with the subject line "Delete my Fund Nest account".
  2. Send it from the email address associated with your organisation's Fund Nest access so we can verify the request comes from the account holder.
  3. We confirm receipt, verify your identity, and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  4. You receive a confirmation email once everything has been removed.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Access credentials — your login access to the Grants Hub service is revoked and removed.
  • Contact details — your name, email address and organisation details held for support and access management.
  • Usage records — any activity records linked to you as an individual.

What is retained, and for how long

DataRetention periodReason
Access credentials, contact details and usage records Deleted within 30 days of a verified request No longer needed
Encrypted backups containing your data Purged on the normal backup rotation, up to 30 days after deletion Disaster recovery
Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics Indefinite Cannot be linked back to you or any individual

Questions?

If you have any questions about deletion or your data, contact us at support@iraady.com before submitting a request — we are happy to help.
